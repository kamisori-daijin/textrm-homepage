textrm

A Language Model optimized for Apple Silicon (MLX), featuring a Tiny Recursive Model architecture.

Why TRM?

  • Latent Recursion: Maximizing reasoning depth within a tiny footprint by recursively iterating through latent states.
  • Deep Supervision: Applying loss at each improvement cycle to ensure stable and coherent <think> processes.
  • Mail-Gen Specialized: Expert-level proficiency in drafting professional emails and polite refusals.

Architecture Overview

Based on the "Tiny Recursive Models" paper, utilizing shared layers to perform n=6 latent recursions and T=3 improvement cycles.

textrm-MoE: Mixture of Experts Edition

textrm-MoE is an advanced extension combining Tiny Recursive Models with Mixture of Experts (MoE) routing, optimized for Apple Silicon.

  • Top-k Routing: Dynamic gating with configurable expert allocation per token.
  • Persistent Shared Expert: A dedicated, non-gated expert always active alongside specialized experts to capture invariant general knowledge.
  • SwiGLU Experts: High-capacity feed-forward expert layers utilizing SwiGLU activations.
  • Adaptive Computation: Integrates a learned Ponder/Halt module to dynamically determine optimal early-exit or accuracy-based execution termination.
  • Dual-Compiled Pipeline: Engineered with strict pure-functional compilation to bypass Metal argument buffer exhaustion and eliminate lazy-evaluation overhead.
  • Zero-Copy Data Pipeline: Pre-tokenized datasets mapped directly into raw binary files, leveraging memory-mapping for instantaneous zero-overhead mini-batching.
  • Deep Supervision & EMA: Multi-step intermediate losses guide recursive state evolution, paired with Exponential Moving Average shadow-weight tracking for superior convergence.
